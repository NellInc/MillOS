# Neuchâtel castle correction

Nell rejected the blue-roofed, gold-spired castle as too Disney and requested Neuchâtel. This authoring pass replaces the architectural language, retaining the landmark location, orientation, footprint and source originals. The height is deliberately reduced. No publishing or audio is authorized.

Official references inspected:
- https://www.neuchatelville.ch/fileadmin/sites/ne_ville/images/40_ans_zone_pietonne/sortir-vieille-ville-chateau-485.jpg
- https://www.neuchatelville.ch/fileadmin/sites/ne_ville/images/SORTIR-Visiter-idees-2.jpg
- https://sitn.ne.ch/patrimoine/detail/1-1951?type=pa01

The church with twin green-roofed spires is the Collégiale, separate from the castle. The game landmark is a reference-led interpretation, not a measured reconstruction.

Accepted concept features: asymmetric connected residential wings; two open courts; lower roof-dominated silhouette; matte russet-brown tiled gables and hips; off-white plaster and warm limestone; rectangular mullioned windows; restrained red/ochre court shutters; an open entrance arch; a small Romanesque gallery; modest stair towers; dormers and chimneys; shallow grounded masonry/rock terrace.

Rejected concept additions: vines, courtyard trees, new terrain or site objects, decorative finials, taller church spires. None is necessary for the requested architectural correction.

Risks: colour-only edits would retain the rejected silhouette; duplicating church spires would copy the wrong building; architectural detail could exceed the unchanged 25,200 vertex / 621,000-byte budgets. Working if the actual delivery has the new massing, courtyard/gateway voids and roof forms, reads correctly from both sides in the live world, and passes its existing resource limits.

Fresh baseline: 25 tests passed in crafted-assets and valleyRelief, zero failures. Previous whole-project suite: 1,946 passed. Self-review: the concepts and subsequent implementation are authored and assessed by the same assistant.
